Privacy Policy

Agave Lane Realty (demo) · Last updated October 23, 2026

Agave Lane Realty is a fictional business. This website and its assistant exist to demonstrate an AI assistant that answers people who message a business. This policy explains what happens to the information you send to it.

What we collect

  • The messages you send and the assistant's and team's replies.
  • The identifier of the app you write from: your phone number on WhatsApp, your user ID and name on Telegram, Messenger or Instagram, or a random session ID in the website chat.
  • Details you choose to share, such as your name, phone number, email address and what you're looking for.

How we use it

  • To reply to you and to show the demo team what you asked for.
  • To keep a record of the conversation in the demo CRM.

We don't sell your information, don't use it for advertising and don't send you marketing messages.

Services that process it

  • Messaging apps you write from: Meta (WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram) and Telegram.
  • AI models that write the replies: Google (Gemini API) and Anthropic (Claude API).
  • HubSpot (CRM) and Slack (team notifications).
  • A server hosted by OVHcloud that stores the conversations.

How long we keep it

This is a demo, so its data is deleted regularly. Conversations and contact records are kept for no longer than 30 days.

Stopping messages and deleting your data

  • Send STOP in the chat and the assistant won't message you again.
  • To have your data deleted sooner, write delete my data in the same chat. A person reads every conversation and will delete your messages and contact record within 7 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
